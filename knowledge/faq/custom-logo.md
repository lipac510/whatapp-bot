# Custom Logo

## Customer questions

- Can you print my logo?
- Do you support custom logo?
- Can I customize the packaging?
- Can you make my own design?

## Short answer

Yes, we support custom logo and custom packaging design. You can send your logo, reference picture, product size, and quantity, and our specialist will help check the best solution.

## Details

- Common customization: logo printing, color, size, material, surface finishing, insert, ribbon, handle.
- If the customer has artwork or a reference photo, ask them to send it.
- If the customer only asks generally, answer briefly and continue collecting product and quantity.
