# Samples

## Customer questions

- What is your sample cost?
- Can I get a sample?
- How long does a sample take?
- What is your sample process?

## Short answer

Yes, we can arrange samples. The sample fee is usually USD 300 per piece. Sample lead time is usually 7-10 days after payment and artwork approval.

## Details

- Sample fee: USD 300 per piece.
- If the final order is 1,000 pcs, the full sampling cost can be deducted.
- If the final order is 500 pcs, 50% of the sampling cost can be deducted.
- Sample lead time: 7-10 days after payment and artwork approval.
- Sample process: confirm requirements, quote and collect payment, approve artwork, produce sample, ship out.
