# Delivery Time

## Customer questions

- How long does production take?
- What is the delivery time?
- How fast can you ship?
- Can I receive it urgently?

## Short answer

Production and delivery time depend on the product, order quantity, customization, and destination country. For rigid boxes, catalog production lead time is usually 15-25 days after design draft confirmation. Samples usually take 7-10 days after payment and artwork approval.

## Details

- Rigid box catalog production lead time: 15-25 days after confirming the design draft.
- Sample lead time: 7-10 days after payment and artwork approval.
- Do not promise exact delivery date without confirming product, order details, and destination country.
- If the customer asks for urgent delivery, collect destination and quantity first, then hand off to Emma if needed.
- If the shipping country is missing, ask for country or full address.

## Assets

- None by default.

## Return prompt

If address is missing, ask: "Where should we ship to?"
