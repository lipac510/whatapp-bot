# Order Process

## Customer questions

- What is your order process?
- How can I place an order?
- What happens after payment?
- Do you do QC before shipping?

## Short answer

Our order process is: order confirmation, collect payment, pre-production sample, mass production, in-process QC, outgoing QC, then shipping and delivery.

## Details

- Order Confirmation
- Collect payment
- Pre-production sample
- Mass production
- In-process QC
- OQC
- Shipping and delivery
