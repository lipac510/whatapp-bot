# MOQ

## Customer questions

- What is your MOQ?
- What is the minimum order quantity?
- Can I order a small quantity?
- Do you accept 100 pcs?

## Short answer

MOQ depends on the product, size, material, and customization. For customized orders, MOQ is usually around 500 pieces. For standard stock designs, MOQ can start from around 100 pieces. Please send the product type and quantity you need, and our specialist will check the best option for you.

## Details

- Customized orders: usually 500 pieces.
- Standard stock designs: usually 100 pieces.
- Rigid box catalog note: MOQ is 100-1000 pcs based on box type and design.
- Do not promise an exact MOQ if the product type or customization is unclear.
- If the customer already gave a product, ask for quantity.
- If the customer already gave quantity, continue collecting address or WhatsApp number.
- For very small quantities, explain that custom production may need a minimum quantity and Emma can confirm options.

## Assets

- None by default.

## Return prompt

Return to the missing collection step: product, quantity, address, or WhatsApp number.
