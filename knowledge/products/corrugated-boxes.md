# Corrugated Boxes

## Customer questions

- Do you make corrugated boxes?
- Can you make shipping boxes?
- Do you have mailer boxes?
- Can you print logo on carton boxes?

## Short answer

Yes, we can make custom corrugated boxes for shipping, mailing, and product packaging. We can support custom size, printing, material strength, and box structure.

## Details

- Common types: shipping cartons, mailer boxes, product boxes, e-commerce boxes.
- Common customization: size, logo printing, color printing, material flute, surface treatment.
- Exact recommendation depends on product size, product weight, quantity, and shipping destination.
