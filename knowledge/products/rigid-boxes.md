# Luxury Rigid Boxes

## Customer questions

- Do you make rigid boxes?
- Can you make luxury gift boxes?
- Do you have magnetic boxes?
- Can I customize a premium box?

## Short answer

Yes, we can make luxury rigid boxes for gift, cosmetic, jewelry, electronics, and premium product packaging. We can support custom size, structure, logo, color, insert, handle, and finishing.

## Details

- Common types: lid and base box, magnetic closure box, drawer box, book-style box.
- Common customization: custom size, logo, paper wrapping, insert tray, ribbon, foil stamping, embossing.
- Catalog box styles include magnetic folding box, magnetic book style box, and drawer box.
- Catalog options include surface finishing, insert options, handle options, and decorative options.
- MOQ is usually 100-1000 pcs depending on box type and design. Customized orders usually start around 500 pcs.
- Production lead time shown in the catalog is 15-25 days after design draft confirmation.
- Exact quotation needs product type, size, quantity, destination, and reference photos if available.
