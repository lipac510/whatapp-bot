# Paper Bags

## Customer questions

- Do you make paper bags?
- Do you have kraft paper bags?
- Can you make shopping bags with logo?
- Do you have paper gift bags?

## Short answer

Yes, we can make custom paper bags for retail, gift, and shopping use. We can support custom size, logo printing, handles, paper material, and surface finishing depending on your requirement.

## Details

- Common types: kraft paper bags, white card paper bags, shopping bags, gift bags.
- Common customization: size, logo, color, handle, paper thickness, surface finishing.
- Exact recommendation depends on product weight, bag size, quantity, and destination country.
